Salad Mixing Lab

Flexible-fiber network dynamics and small-disk statistics under alternating sine flow in a periodic box.

Model and equations of motion

A dimensionless two-dimensional flexible-fiber and disk discrete-element model. Each fiber is a chain of nodes connected by elastic bonds. Different fibers and disks interact through repulsive contact forces and history-dependent tangential friction. The network is emergent: no bonds are added between different fibers.

Alternating sine-flow drive

\[ \mathbf u(\mathbf r,t)= \begin{cases} \left(\dfrac{WK}{\tau}\sin\dfrac{2\pi y}{H},\,0\right), & 0\leq t\bmod 2\tau<\tau,\\[6pt] \left(0,\,\dfrac{HK}{\tau}\sin\dfrac{2\pi x}{W}\right), & \tau\leq t\bmod 2\tau<2\tau . \end{cases} \]

The domain is periodic with \(W=18\), \(H=12\), and switching interval \(\tau=2.5\). Each node or disk is driven toward the prescribed local velocity through linear drag. The flow is imposed, not obtained from solving Navier–Stokes.

Elastic energy of a fiber

\[ E_s=\frac{k_s}{2}\sum_{a=1}^{M-1}\left(\left|\mathbf r_{a+1}-\mathbf r_a\right|-\ell_0\right)^2,\qquad E_b=\frac{\kappa}{2}\sum_{a=2}^{M-1}\left|\mathbf r_{a-1}-2\mathbf r_a+\mathbf r_{a+1}\right|^2. \]

The elastic forces are computed from \(-\nabla(E_s+E_b)\), with minimum-image bond displacements. \(k_s=400\) is fixed, while \(\kappa\) is controlled by the bending-stiffness slider. This is a discrete curvature penalty, not a calibrated thin-sheet bending modulus. All fibers have five nodes.

Translational dynamics

\[ m_a\ddot{\mathbf r}_a =-\frac{\partial(E_s+E_b)}{\partial\mathbf r_a} +\sum_{b\ne a}\mathbf F_{ab}^{\mathrm{contact}} +\gamma m_a\left[\mathbf u(\mathbf r_a,t)-\dot{\mathbf r}_a\right]. \]

For independent disks the elastic term vanishes. Here \(\gamma=8\). Interspecies contact forces act on individual nodes, allowing fibers to bend around obstacles. No tensile attraction or permanent cross-fiber links are used.

Normal and history-dependent tangential contact

\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_{ab}&=R_a+R_b-d_{ab},\\ F_n&=\max\left(0,k_n\delta_{ab}-\eta_n v_n\right),\quad\delta_{ab}>0,\\ \dot{\xi}_{ab}&=v_t,\qquad F_t^{\mathrm{trial}}=-k_t\xi_{ab}-\eta_t v_t,\\ F_t&=\operatorname{clip}\left(F_t^{\mathrm{trial}},-\mu F_n,\mu F_n\right),\\ \mathbf F_{ab}&=F_n\hat{\mathbf n}+F_t\hat{\mathbf t}. \end{aligned} \]

The tangential spring displacement \(\xi_{ab}\) persists while a node–node or disk–node contact remains active; it is projected as the contact tangent rotates and reset when the contact breaks. On sliding, the memory is corrected to enforce the Coulomb limit. Parameters: \(k_n=650\), \(\eta_n=10\), \(k_t=130\), \(\eta_t=2\). Contact is implemented between nodes, so it approximates each fiber as a bead-chain, not as a continuous nonpenetrable ribbon.

What the measurements mean

\[ G(t)=\frac{N_{\mathrm{largest\ connected\ fiber\ cluster}}(t)}{N_f}, \qquad S_{dd}(\mathbf k)=\frac{1}{N_d}\left|\sum_{j=1}^{N_d}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r_j}\right|^2. \]

Connectivity is based on instantaneous contacts between different fibers. Contact age records the current uninterrupted lifetime of a node-pair contact. A large connected component alone does not prove mechanical rigidity. The structure factor is radially averaged and sampled after \(t=10\). Frictional history can support shear loads, but our two-dimensional node-chain approximation still cannot reproduce the full over-and-under geometry of three-dimensional flexible leaves.

Numerical method and limitations

The equations are integrated with an explicit semi-implicit Euler update at \(\Delta t=0.002\). A periodic cell grid accelerates short-range contacts; velocity caps are used as a safety measure. The model has not been calibrated against real leaves, and numerical convergence or system-size independence has not yet been demonstrated. For ideal noninteracting tracers the exact one-cycle map is \(X'=X+K\sin(2\pi Y)\), \(Y'=Y+K\sin(2\pi X')\), modulo one; interacting fibers and disks need not follow it.

Observables

Time-averaged disk structure factor Sdd(k)

Averaging starts after t = 10.

The mixing metric is the normalized variance of disk counts in a 4 × 3 spatial grid. For a finite number of randomly distributed disks, it fluctuates above zero. Disk–rod, rod–rod, and disk–disk contacts are enabled.

Changing a parameter requires a new run or Restart same seed. Fibers are five-node chains. Different fibers are never permanently linked.
