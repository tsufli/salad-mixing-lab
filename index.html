Salad Mixing Lab

Rigid rods and small disks with contact interactions under a single alternating sine-flow protocol. The box is periodic: particles exiting one side re-enter on the opposite side.

The mixing metric is the normalized variance of disk counts in a 4 × 3 spatial grid. For a finite number of randomly distributed disks, it fluctuates above zero. Disk–rod, rod–rod, and disk–disk contacts are enabled.

Each half-period lasts 2.5 time units. In normalized coordinates, passive tracers obey X' = X + K sin(2πY), then Y' = Y + K sin(2πX'), modulo 1.

The rods/disks have inertia, contacts and drag: their trajectories are not exactly this tracer map. Changes take effect on the next run. Use Restart same seed to compare friction.
